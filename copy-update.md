# PART 1 — THE BRIEF

## 1.1 Who EdComrade Is

EdComrade is a Ghanaian educational support organisation that helps private schools across Africa do two things well:

1. **Get found and trusted by families** — through digital presence, verified profiles, and professional presentation.
2. **Run their operations efficiently** — through school management software (ERP), a parent communication app (ParentAide), and a school discovery platform (Schoolpedia).

EdComrade operates through focused campaigns. The two active ones are:
- **Pioneers Programme** — Free 6-month access to the full software suite for selected schools. Limited slots per region.
- **Digital 100** — A GHS 5,000 one-time package covering professional photography, video, website, Google Business Profile, and Schoolpedia listing. Limited to 100 schools nationwide.

The primary customers are **private school proprietors and heads** in Ghana. Secondary audiences are **parents** searching for schools and using the ParentAide app.

EdComrade is early-stage and building its reputation. Honesty and trust are core to the brand.

---

## 1.2 The Problem With the Current Site

The current site has five specific problems the rewrite must fix:

**Problem 1 — Wrong audience voice.**
The homepage speaks to investors and industry observers ("$50B private education crisis," "systemic intelligence," "total inevitability") rather than to school leaders who need practical help. Every page must now speak directly to its primary audience — school heads and proprietors — in plain, confident language.

**Problem 2 — Broken trust signals.**
The stats block shows "0+ Schools," "0% Retention," "0M+ Records." This communicates that nothing is live. Remove the stats block entirely OR replace it with honest, forward-looking framing. Do not show metrics that are zero or appear to be build errors.

**Problem 3 — Pricing contradiction.**
The Digital 100 page says schools pay "₵0" and "Zero Development Cost." The actual price is GHS 5,000. This must be corrected. The page should clearly state the GHS 5,000 investment and frame the value accurately.

**Problem 4 — Wrong form on the For Parents page.**
The For Parents page ends with a form asking for "Institution Name" and "Admin/Owner Name" — these are school fields. A parent landing on this page should not see a school admin form. Replace with a parent-appropriate action (see copy below).

**Problem 5 — "Inevitability" overuse.**
The word "inevitable" or "inevitability" appears four times across the site as a brand concept. It reads as internal founder language, not customer language. Remove all instances and replace with outcome-based language.

---

## 1.4 Decision Rules for the Agent

When the brief does not cover something explicitly, the agent should follow these rules:

1. **Default to the school audience.** When in doubt about tone or framing, ask: would a busy school proprietor in Ghana find this clear and useful?
2. **Plain language over jargon.** Replace any technical or abstract language with simple, direct equivalents. "ERP" → "school management system." "Interoperability" → "works together."
3. **Honest over aspirational.** Do not add claims that cannot be verified. Do not add testimonials, case studies, or metrics unless they appear in this brief.
4. **Preserve navigation.** Do not change page URLs, nav structure, or link targets unless a specific fix is noted in this brief.
5. **Keep CTAs action-oriented.** Every CTA should start with a verb and describe the outcome. "Apply Now" is good. "Learn about Inevitability" is not.
