  **3. The Pioneers Platform --- What It Does**

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

Pioneers is a school management platform --- a set of tools that handles
every aspect of running your school, accessible from a computer or a
smartphone. You do not need any technical background to use it. You do
not need to install anything complicated. You log in, and everything
your school needs is there.

The platform is built in modules. Think of it like a set of tools on a
shelf --- you pick the ones your school needs, and you only pay for
those. Every school starts with Core Admin, which is the foundation.
Everything else is optional.

listing at no additional charge. This is not a basic directory entry. It
